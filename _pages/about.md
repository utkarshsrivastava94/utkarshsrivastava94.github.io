At **EOG Resources**, I architect and maintain SCADA solutions using Ignition and CygNet for real-time monitoring of field assets, while integrating MLOps workflows for predictive maintenance and anomaly detection on sensor data.

Previously at **Charles Schwab**, I built high-throughput data collection systems — including the ThousandEyes Collector, which executes 3,000+ API calls per minute streaming telemetry to Kafka — along with Django-based server readiness platforms, Venafi certificate automation, and 100+ SALT infrastructure scripts across hybrid-cloud environments.

My academic background in Petroleum Engineering and Chemical Engineering (BITS Pilani) gives me a strong foundation in energy markets, reservoir modeling, and optimization — areas I continue to explore through side projects in electricity load forecasting and quantitative energy modeling.
