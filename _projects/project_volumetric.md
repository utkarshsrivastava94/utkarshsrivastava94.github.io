### Overview

A volumetric and decline analysis study using Surfer to estimate encroached hydrocarbons and predict future production across a 25-well dataset.

### Key Deliverables

- **Volumetric Analysis:** Conducted volumetric analysis on 25 wells in Surfer to estimate encroached oil and gas volumes in the reservoir.
- **Material Balance:** Performed material balance calculations to estimate recovery factors and estimated ultimate recovery (EUR) at low reservoir pressures.
- **Decline Curve Analysis:** Conducted Decline Curve Analysis (DCA) on 16 years of historical production data to forecast future production trajectories.

### Tech Stack

`Surfer` · `Volumetric Analysis` · `Material Balance` · `Decline Curve Analysis (DCA)` · `EUR Estimation`
