### Overview

A comprehensive volumetric and decline analysis study using Surfer to map hydrocarbon distribution across a 25-well dataset, estimate recovery factors, and forecast long-term production trajectories using 16 years of historical data.

### Key Achievements

- **Volumetric Analysis:** Conducted volumetric analysis on **25 wells** in Surfer to construct subsurface maps and estimate encroached oil and gas volumes in the reservoir, providing spatial insight into hydrocarbon distribution.
- **Material Balance Calculations:** Performed material balance calculations to estimate recovery factors and **Estimated Ultimate Recovery (EUR)** at low reservoir pressures, accounting for drive mechanism and depletion behavior.
- **Decline Curve Analysis:** Conducted **Decline Curve Analysis (DCA)** on **16 years** of historical production data using exponential, hyperbolic, and harmonic decline models to predict future production rates and field economic life.

### Tech Stack

`Surfer` · `Volumetric Analysis` · `Material Balance` · `Decline Curve Analysis (DCA)` · `EUR Estimation` · `Subsurface Mapping`
