### Overview

A petroleum engineering project using Techlog to analyze an exploratory well and assess the economic viability of future drilling operations in the study area.

### Key Deliverables

- **Well Log Analysis:** Analyzed well logs to determine essential OOIP parameters including porosity, permeability, lithology, and water saturation using Techlog.
- **Pay Zone Identification:** Utilized log data to identify pay zone depths and thickness for both exploratory and infill wells.
- **Economic Analysis:** Performed ROI calculations and economic analysis to evaluate the viability of future drilling operations and recommend go/no-go decisions.

### Tech Stack

`Techlog` · `Well Log Analysis` · `OOIP Estimation` · `Reservoir Characterization` · `Economic Modeling`
