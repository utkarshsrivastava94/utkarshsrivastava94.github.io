### Overview

A reservoir characterization and economic feasibility study using Techlog to analyze an exploratory well and determine whether future drilling operations in the study area are economically viable.

### Key Achievements

- **OOIP Parameter Estimation:** Analyzed an exploratory well using Techlog to determine essential parameters for Original Oil In Place (OOIP) estimation — including porosity, permeability, lithology, and water saturation — from raw well log data.
- **Pay Zone Identification:** Utilized well logs to identify pay zone depths and net thickness for both the exploratory well and proposed infill well locations, informing future field development decisions.
- **Economic Analysis:** Performed ROI calculations and economic analysis incorporating capital costs, production forecasts, and commodity price assumptions to evaluate the viability of future drilling operations and provide a go/no-go recommendation.

### Tech Stack

`Techlog` · `Well Log Interpretation` · `OOIP Estimation` · `Reservoir Characterization` · `Economic Modeling` · `ROI Analysis`
