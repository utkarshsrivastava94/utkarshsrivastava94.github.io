### Overview

A national inter-university engineering competition where student teams design, build, and race a single-seat off-road all-terrain vehicle (ATV). Our vehicle was named **"SHARK"**.

### Key Contributions

- **Steering System:** Designed a centrally mounted steering column with a tilting provision using pillow block bearings for improved driver ergonomics and control.
- **Suspension Design:** Designed a double wishbone suspension system with dampers for both the front and rear of the vehicle, optimizing stability and ride quality on rough terrain.
- **Assembly:** Participated in the full assembly and testing cycle of the ATV, from component fabrication to track validation.

### Tech Stack

`Mechanical Design` · `Double Wishbone Suspension` · `Steering Systems` · `ATV Engineering` · `CAD`
