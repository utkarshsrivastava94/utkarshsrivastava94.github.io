### Overview

SAE BAJA is a national inter-university engineering competition where student teams design, fabricate, and race a single-seat off-road all-terrain vehicle (ATV) from scratch. Our vehicle — named **"SHARK"** — was engineered for rugged terrain performance with a focus on structural integrity, handling, and driver safety.

### Key Contributions

- **Vehicle Assembly:** Participated in the full design and assembly lifecycle of a workable ATV model, from concept and CAD design through fabrication, integration, and track validation.
- **Steering System Design:** Designed a centrally mounted steering column with a tilting provision using **pillow block bearings**, improving driver ergonomics, adjustability, and steering precision under off-road conditions.
- **Suspension Design:** Designed a **double wishbone suspension system** with dampers for both the front and rear of the vehicle, optimizing wheel articulation, load transfer, and ride stability on rough terrain.

### Tech Stack
