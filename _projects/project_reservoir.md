### Overview

A production optimization study for the San Joaquin Basin combining SPOTFIRE data analytics, PIPESIM flow simulations, and MATLAB waterflooding modeling to identify economic production strategies.

### Key Deliverables

- **Production Data Analysis:** Analyzed production data using SPOTFIRE to identify operator and decline rates, generating P10, P50, and P90 reserve classifications.
- **PIPESIM Simulations:** Ran PIPESIM flow simulations to identify production-enhancing parameters and test the economic viability of intervention scenarios.
- **Waterflooding Simulation:** Executed a 5-point waterflooding MATLAB simulation to determine waterflooding effectiveness and optimized production tubing diameter for maximum recovery.

### Tech Stack

`SPOTFIRE` · `PIPESIM` · `MATLAB` · `Decline Curve Analysis` · `Reservoir Simulation` · `Production Optimization`
