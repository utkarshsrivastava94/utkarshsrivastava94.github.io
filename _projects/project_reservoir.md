### Overview

A production optimization study for the San Joaquin Basin combining SPOTFIRE analytics, PIPESIM flow assurance simulations, and MATLAB waterflooding modeling to identify production-enhancing strategies and validate their economic viability.

### Key Achievements

- **Production Data Analysis:** Analyzed field production data using SPOTFIRE to identify operator decline rates and generate probabilistic reserve estimates — **P10, P50, and P90** classifications — providing a statistical basis for development planning.
- **PIPESIM Flow Simulations:** Performed PIPESIM nodal analysis simulations to identify production-enhancing parameters such as tubing diameter, wellhead pressure, and artificial lift configuration, and tested the economic viability of each intervention scenario.
- **Waterflooding Simulation:** Executed a **5-point waterflooding MATLAB simulation** to determine waterflooding sweep efficiency and optimize production tubing diameter for maximum oil recovery rate under secondary recovery conditions.
